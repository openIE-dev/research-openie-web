### 5.5 The model as a proposer behind the check

A model may propose equivalences. It may not admit them. With a local 1.5-billion-parameter coding model (Qwen2.5-Coder-1.5B under llama.cpp) on the demonstration specification, the proposer offered 16 equivalences. The check admitted 3, by randomized trials and a Z3 proof, and rejected 13. A deterministic proposer of 87 familiar identities, 77 arithmetic and 10 structural, had 9 admitted, all structural, and 78 rejected: every arithmetic identity, for fault order, non-numbers or overflow, and one structural rewrite, `const-absorbs-pipe`, which would drop an escalating prefix (engine test `tests/form_proposers.rs`).
