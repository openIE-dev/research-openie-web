# OpenIE Research Radio & Global Rung Radar — Autonomous 6h Commons-Grounded Engine

`scripts/radio/` implements the six-stage autonomous loop backing `/radio/`, `/radio/rss.xml`, `/media/radio/wai/*.wai`, `/data/global-rung-radar.json`, and the Global Rung Radar on `avalanche.openie.dev/forecast/`:

1. **`scraper.py` — Sanctioned Open-Access Literature Intake**
   Polls documented public scientific APIs (`arXiv`, `OpenAlex`, `Europe PMC`, and `bioRxiv`) across seven research fronts spanning agentic tool use, formal `Lean 4` verification, `FPGA`/`LUT` lookup fabrics, memory-mapped case stores, energy-proportional computing, multi-agent protocols, and quorum signaling. Deduplicates entries via `BLAKE2b-256`, `SHA-256`, normalized `arXiv` stems, and `DOI` keys.
2. **`commons_prior_art.py` — Blueprint §6 Prior-Art Pre-Check (`scholar.askdavidc.ai`)**
   Queries live `https://scholar.askdavidc.ai/api/ask` and `POST https://scholar.askdavidc.ai/mcp` (`commons_prior_art`) across `1,991` CC0 prior-art disclosures plus indexed Research TV/Radio transcripts before any novel synthesis continuation is proposed, recording `commons_corroboration` (`hit_count`, `top_citation_url`, `top_statement_excerpt`, `checked_at_utc`) into `radio-radar.json`.
3. **`rung_radar.py` & `reviewer.py` — 9-Rung Avalanche Classifier & Epistemic Cross-Examiner**
   Projects every external work onto the **9-Rung Computer Intelligence Avalanche & Compilation Threshold** (`R1 Search` .. `R5 Tool Use` → *Compilation Threshold* `ΔT > ΔT* ∧ C(z) = 1` → `R6 Immersion` .. `R9 Economy`), assigning `threshold_regime`, `target_tier`, plain-English takeaways with zero unexplained acronyms, formal mechanisms, `Shape D` falsifier tests, `[T]`/`[E]`/`[M]`/`[S]` epistemic badges, and OpenIE study links.
4. **`foundry_pilots.py` & `foundry_runner.py` — Commons-Grounded Candidate Topic Foundry & `<60s` Pilot Loop**
   Runs `commons_prior_art_check(query)` ahead of every candidate research topic, executes deterministic Python pilots under 60 seconds with wrap-safe Linux `powercap` `intel-rapl:0` `reported_j` telemetry (`board_metered=false`), and attaches both `commons_corroboration` and pilot verdicts into `radio-radar.json`.
5. **`personas.py`, `studio_synth.py`, `wai_envelope.py` & `produce_radio_ep16.py` — Global Studio Ensemble & `WAI1` Binary Envelopes**
   Voices multi-persona roundtables using `ElevenLabs eleven_v4` (`mp3_44100_192`, SHA-256 cached in `state/eleven_cache/`), masters `44.1 kHz`, `128 kbps` Stereo AAC `.m4a` (`-16 LUFS`, `+faststart`) alongside `.vtt` WebVTT captions and `.json` transcripts, and emits byte-conforming `WAI1` (`open-standards/wai/SPEC.md` §2–§3) binary envelopes (`public/media/radio/wai/<episode_id>.wai`) sealed with `moq-jwp` `BLAKE3` Merkle roots and `Ed25519` signatures (`receipts.json`).
6. **`radio_daemon.py` & `systemd/` — 6-Hour Autonomous Linux Scheduler**
   Protected by non-blocking `fcntl.flock`, scheduled every 6 hours via `systemd --user` (`openie-research-radio.timer`, `Persistent=true`). Run manually via `python radio_daemon.py --once`. Reads `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY`, runtime paths, and `OPENIE_RADIO_SYNC_TARGET` strictly from `~/.config/openie-radio/env` (`chmod 600`) outside version control.
