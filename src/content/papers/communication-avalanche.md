## 1. Thesis and the Conserved Externalize-Ingest-Transform Operator

Across biological evolution, cultural history, and distributed computation, isolated agent capacity encounters steep metabolic and thermodynamic diminishing returns long before collective task complexity saturates. What repeatedly unlocks superlinear capability is a **communication tool avalanche**: a conserved three-phase operator in which agents write internal state into an external physical substrate, neighboring agents selectively ingest that substrate only when an activation threshold and validity gate are satisfied, and ingested signals transform recipient state while preserving prior verified gains (`Figure 1`).

Let a population of $N$ agents carry local states $s_i(t) \in \mathcal{S}$ ($i \in \{1,\dots,N\}$) coupled to a shared physical or digital medium $\mathcal{M}$. Each rung of the avalanche instantiates three maps (`[T]`, formalized in `Avalanche.QuorumRatchet.CycleOperator`):

1. **Externalization ($\mathcal{E} : \mathcal{S} \to \Sigma$)**: Agent $i$ expends bounded signaling energy $e_{\text{ext}} > 0$ to emit a structured physical artifact or packet $m = \mathcal{E}(s_i)$ into $\mathcal{M}$ (an acyl-homoserine lactone molecule, a slime sheath, an acoustic contour, a printed equation, or a `BLAKE3`-hashed, `Ed25519`-signed channel payload).
2. **Thresholded and Gated Ingestion ($\mathcal{G} : \mathcal{S} \times \Sigma^* \to \{0,1\}$)**: Recipient $j$ accumulates weighted incoming signals $A_j = \sum_k w_{kj}\,m_k$ and fires if and only if $A_j \ge \theta_j$ and receptor/verifier specificity $V_j(m_k) = 1$ holds:
   $$s_j(t + 1) = \begin{cases} \mathcal{T}\!\left(s_j(t),\, \{m_k\}\right) & \text{if } \sum_k w_{kj}\,\mathbb{I}[V_j(m_k)=1] \ge \theta_j, \\ s_j(t) & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$
   Sub-threshold arrivals ($A_j < \theta_j$) and unverified payloads ($V_j(m_k) = 0$) trigger zero downstream state transitions (`CTA-T7`, `CTA-T8`, `CTA-T17`, `CTA-T18`).
3. **Ratchet Transformation and Downward Crystallization ($\mathcal{T} : \mathcal{S} \times \Sigma^* \to \mathcal{S}$)**: Accepted signals update both collective coordination and individual competence. When validated artifacts persist in $\mathcal{M}$ with quality non-regression $Q(s_j(t+1)) \ge Q(s_j(t))$, high-energy exploratory discoveries become low-cost reusable templates for subsequent generations (`CTA-T5`, `CTA-T6`, `CTA-T23`).

Without a selective gate $V_j$, a relay chain of $k$ hops with per-hop fidelity $f \in (0, 1)$ decays exponentially as $f^k$ (`CTA-T2`), and additive noise $\epsilon > 0$ grows linearly as $k\epsilon$ (`CTA-T1`). With a sound verification gate and persistent externalized storage, quality becomes a monotonically non-decreasing ratchet (`CTA-T5`). To compare coordination architectures across biological and silicon substrates on equal footing, let $Q \ge 0$ denote task-verified coherent output (such as test-verified bug repairs, retained metabolic viability, or error-free transmitted bits) and $E_{\text{total}} = E_{\text{compute}} + E_{\text{comm}} + E_{\text{idle}} > 0$ denote total expended energy. **Coherence per Joule** is defined as:

$$\mathrm{CpJ} = \frac{Q}{E_{\text{compute}} + E_{\text{comm}} + E_{\text{idle}}}\;\; \left[\text{verified coherence units} \cdot \mathrm{J}^{-1}\right].$$

Raw component count (`5,570` bacterial open reading frames, $8.6 \times 10^{10}$ human neurons, or $2 \times 10^{12}$ frontier neural-network weights) sets only an upper capacity envelope; realized $\mathrm{CpJ}$ is governed by whether communication channels are thresholded, event-driven, and gated against downstream error cascades (`CTA-T19` to `CTA-T23`).

### Worked Example 2: Historical Ratchet Diagnosis (`1950`–`2026`) — Why `1975` `MYCIN` and `1980` `Hearsay-II` Stalled into AI Winter I While `2016` `AlphaGo` and `2024` `FunSearch` Ignited (`CTA-T1`–`CTA-T6`)

Mapping historical artificial intelligence architectures onto the three-phase operator $(\mathcal{E}, \mathcal{G}, \mathcal{T})$ explains why early multi-module systems stalled while modern generate-and-verify loops achieved super-human lock-in (`[E]`, `[T]`):

1. **Why `MYCIN` (`1975`) and `Hearsay-II` (`1980`) Stalled**:
   - *Externalization ($\mathcal{E}$)*: `MYCIN` externalized `~450` hand-authored `Lisp` production rules carrying subjective Certainty Factors (`CF`, $\mathrm{CF} \in [-1, 1]$); `Hearsay-II` externalized multi-granular speech hypotheses (acoustic, phonetic, syllabic, lexical, phrasal) onto a global shared blackboard polled by $K$ concurrent Knowledge Sources (`KS`).
   - *Unsound Gate ($\mathcal{G}$)*: Neither architecture possessed an exact truth-preserving admission gate ($V(m) = 1 \not\Rightarrow \text{valid}(m)$). Because `MYCIN`'s `CF` combination algebra treated correlated clinical evidence as conditionally independent without directed acyclic graph (`DAG`) `d`-separation, chaining $k$ rules accumulated calibration drift $\mathrm{err}_0 + k\epsilon$ (`CTA-T1`) and suffered multiplicative fidelity decay $f^k$ (`CTA-T2`: at per-hop rule precision $f = 0.92$ across $k = 12$ chained rules, joint validity drops to $0.92^{12} = 0.3677$). Simultaneously, `Hearsay-II`'s unthresholded blackboard forced $O(K \cdot |B|)$ scheduler checks across noisy bottom-up syllable fragments (`CTA-T9`), spending the majority of compute arbitrations on false-positive partial parses.
   - *Missing Downward Distillation ($\mathcal{T}$)*: Extending coverage from `450` to `4,500` rules required human specialists to audit pairwise rule interactions manually, stalling into **AI Winter I** when maintenance energy outgrew diagnostic yield.
2. **Why `AlphaGo` (`2016`) and `FunSearch` (`2024`) Ignited**:
   - *Externalization ($\mathcal{E}$)*: `AlphaGo` externalized learned policy and value priors $(p_\theta, v_\theta)$ into an explicit Monte Carlo Tree Search (`MCTS`) state-action table $(N(s,a), Q(s,a))$; `FunSearch` externalized compact Python priority functions (`10` to `30` lines of code, not natural-language essays) into a multi-island evolutionary program database.
   - *Sound Deterministic Gate ($\mathcal{G}$)*: `AlphaGo` gated legal transitions and terminal outcomes via exact Go board rules; `FunSearch` gated every language-model mutation through a deterministic combinatorial witness checker (verifying exact cap-set independence or bin-packing capacity in milliseconds). Any hallucinated or sub-optimal code received `admittedWeight = 0` (`CTA-T3`, `CTA-T17`), keeping island contamination strictly at `0.0%` even when >`99%` of raw model samples were non-improving.
   - *Non-Regressing Lock-In ($\mathcal{T}$)*: `AlphaGo` distilled expensive multi-thousand-rollout `MCTS` distributions downward into fast single-pass neural weights, while `FunSearch` retained strictly higher-scoring verified programs ($Q(t+1) \ge Q(t)$, `CTA-T5`, `CTA-T6`) across islands — converting noisy stochastic proposals into a permanent mathematical ratchet.

## 5. Event-Driven Attested Orchestration, Five-Host Fleet Telemetry, and Ten-Camp Synthesis (`CTA-E1` to `CTA-E5`)

Biological coordination across every evolutionary rung surveyed in Sections 2 and 3 runs on local concentration gradients, cooperative activation thresholds, and selective pore sealing rather than global clock ticks or unverified broadcast chatter. Translating those mechanisms into software engineering yields an event-driven orchestration architecture in which heterogeneous compute kernels and language models communicate over energy-budgeted, cryptographically attested channels. This section specifies the four structural components of the Communication Tool Avalanche (CTA) orchestrator, states the pre-registered falsifiers (`F1` to `F6`, locked prior to execution under Secure Hash Algorithm `SHA-256` digest `5739bcdd6c35662833a470bc56bd64f991774c2d920a5a9c059c0acebdf55e7f`), and reports executed hardware measurements across three experiments (`CTA-E1`, `CTA-E2`, and `CTA-E3`) on a 20-thread `13th Gen Intel Core i9-13900H` Linux host (`rustc 1.100.0`, `Python 3.14.4`).

### 5.1 Measurement Grammar and Telemetry Disclosure

All local processor energy values in `CTA-E1` through `CTA-E3` are read from Linux `sysfs` `powercap` Running Average Power Limit (RAPL) counters using wrap-safe 64-bit accumulation modulo `max_energy_range_uj = 262,143,328,850` microjoules (`262.143 J`). Every measurement adheres strictly to three separation rules:

1. **On-chip `reported_j` versus external wall power**: No external inline shunt or alternating-current (AC) board power analyzer was attached (`measured_j = None`, `board_metered = false`). All local energy figures are stamped `reported_j`.
2. **Three-rail RAPL disclosure (`package-0`, `core`, and `psys`)**: Every experiment records `intel-rapl:0` (`package-0`, central processing unit (CPU) and system-on-chip (SoC) socket energy `pkg_reported_j`), `intel-rapl:0:0` (`core`, processor core plane `core_reported_j`), and `intel-rapl:1` (`psys`, platform counter `psys_reported_j`). On this laptop host, original equipment manufacturer (OEM) Embedded Controller (EC) firmware leaves `intel-rapl:1` (`psys`) at a static quiescent rate of `~0.202 W` (`~0.20 J/s`) that does not scale when `package-0` rises from `~5.0 W` idle to `~27.7 W` under single-core load. Primary local Coherence per Joule (CpJ) is therefore computed against dynamic `pkg_reported_j` (and reported alongside `core_reported_j`), while `psys_reported_j` is preserved verbatim in every JSON artifact and ledger line.
3. **Strict separation of local joules from remote cloud tokens**: When an arm invokes a remote Application Programming Interface (API) large language model (LLM), remote datacenter server energy is recorded strictly as `not_available` and never imputed from heuristic constants. Exact API call counts, prompt and completion token counts, and United States dollars (USD) at public list rates (`\$3.00` per million input tokens and `\$15.00` per million output tokens for `Claude Sonnet 5.5`) are reported in dedicated columns alongside local `pkg_reported_j`.

### 5.2 Orchestrator Architecture, Attested Channels, and Min-Cost Flow

Let $G = (V, E)$ denote a directed acyclic graph (DAG) of execution nodes $u \in V$ connected by typed directed channels $(i, u) \in E$:

1. **Heterogeneous execution pool and `cost_lut.json`**: Deterministic nodes execute lexical and abstract syntax tree (AST) parsing (`parse_kernel`), scope, mutable-default, and type-coercion analysis (`static_type_scope_kernel`), docstring and algebraic invariant synthesis (`test_gen_kernel`), bounded AST/token repair (`det_patch_kernel`), and isolated behavioral admission (`diff_verifier_kernel`). Nondeterministic synthesis (`llm_patch_proposer`) sits on a high-cost escalation branch. Prior to orchestration runs, every deterministic kernel and cryptographic primitive is profiled across three input-size buckets (`< 320 B`, `320-640 B`, `> 640 B`) into a hardware-keyed lookup table (`cost_lut.json`).
2. **Constant-time cryptographic channel attestation**: Every channel edge carries an envelope signed by the sender's deterministic `Ed25519` keypair (`64`-byte Request for Comments (RFC) `8032` strict signature via `ed25519-dalek`) over a domain-separated header and the `256`-bit `BLAKE3` digest of the payload bytes. Receiving nodes check sender authorization, constant-time `BLAKE3` digest equality, strict `Ed25519` signature validity, and JavaScript Object Notation (JSON) frame completeness before mutating node state.
3. **Successive Shortest Path (SSP) min-cost flow scheduler**: Given edge capacities $c(i, u)$ and microjoule-scaled weights $w(i, u)$ drawn from `cost_lut.json`, the compiled Rust scheduler computes a minimum-cost flow of demand $D$ from source $s$ to commit sink $t$:

$$
\min_{f} \sum_{(i, u) \in E} w(i, u)\, f(i, u) \quad \text{subject to} \quad 0 \le f(i, u) \le c(i, u), \quad \sum_{i} f(i, u) - \sum_{v} f(u, v) = b(u),
$$

using Bellman-Ford initial node potentials $\pi(u)$ followed by Johnson reduced-cost Dijkstra augmentations $w_{\pi}(i, u) = w(i, u) + \pi(i) - \pi(u) \ge 0$. Because the schedule depends only on graph topology and `cost_lut.json`, `SSP` executes once at topology epoch `0` (`2.11 µs` for the `CTA-E2` pool) and recomputes solely when a node drops out or joins (`0` per-tick scheduler overhead).
4. **Thresholded integrate-and-fire wakeup (zero clocks, zero polling)**: Each node $u$ maintains an accumulated membrane potential $V_u$ over incoming weighted signals $s_i$:

$$
V_u(t) = \sum_{(i, u) \in E} w_{iu}\, s_i(t), \qquad \text{fire}(u) \iff V_u(t) \ge \theta_u.
$$

Worker threads block in the Linux kernel via `eventfd` (`EFD_CLOEXEC`), consuming zero CPU cycles while $V_u < \theta_u$. Upstream node $i$ writes to `eventfd` strictly at the threshold-crossing transition $V_u < \theta_u \to V_u \ge \theta_u$. Every firing appends an immutable receipt `(trace_id, step_idx, node_id, input_blake3, output_blake3, ed25519_sig_prefix, verify_ns, psys_reported_j, pkg_reported_j, latency_ms, potential_v, threshold_theta)` to the energy ledger.
