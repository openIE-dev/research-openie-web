# RCE-JEPA: World Coding, with JEPA as Premise

RCE-JEPA (RCE is the Reality Code Engine; JEPA is a Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) keeps its name so the work stays findable. The subject is World Coding. JEPA is the premise: a learned model predicts a compact summary of a program instead of writing the next word-piece of text. A World of Code is the space of canonical program Forms plus the exact transition and acceptance rules (the Laws of Coding). The proposer slot is any learned model. The checker decides. Hypotheses H1 to H15 are stated in the paper. Measured results now cover H1 (median description-length ratio 9.34 over 185 hand-written programs), H2 (`C_check / E_model = 0.000582 <= 0.05`, `1,718` times cheaper on Apple M5 Max in on-chip `reported_j`), H7 fuzzed arm (0 invariant violations among 1,275 mutants), H8 (0 replay mismatches on 10,500 streams across six targets), H14 (deterministic ladder closing 111 of 151 tasks, 73.5%, at zero model calls, leaving 40 residual tasks), H15 plus Shape D (`DNULLMM1`) Gear-0 and the 3-Gear Cascade across those 40 residual tasks (`17/40` to `34/40` admitted across off-the-shelf cloud and local proposers, `22/40` at `k = 1` and `27/40` at `k = 16` for the first purpose-trained `8.42M`-parameter `FormJEPA-v1` latent Form repair proposer at `58.65 mJ` / `474.76 mJ` RAPL core `reported_j`, `34/40` Gear-0 replay at `0` calls and `0.325 µs` per lookup, and `8.70×` fewer frontier calls under a `Zipf(0.85)` stream), and the 151-task `rce repair` rung `f` promotion audit (`3/119 = 2.5% > 2.0%`, `3/3` passing `W-MUT-1`, `0` contradictions, confirming rung `f` stays in `observe` mode). Full intent-to-Form synthesis (H3), calibrated multi-term energy ranking (H5, H11), and external wall-meter energy (`measured_j`) remain unset.
