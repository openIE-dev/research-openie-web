# RCE-JEPA: World Coding, with JEPA as Premise

RCE-JEPA (RCE is the Reality Code Engine; JEPA is a Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) keeps its name so the work stays findable. The subject is World Coding. JEPA is the premise: a learned model predicts a compact summary of a program instead of writing the next word-piece of text. A World of Code is the space of canonical program Forms plus the exact transition and acceptance rules (the Laws of Coding). The proposer slot is any learned model. The checker decides. Hypotheses H1 to H15 are stated in the paper. Measured results now cover H1 (median description-length ratio 9.34 over 185 hand-written programs), H2 (`C_check / E_model = 0.000582 <= 0.05`, `1,718` times cheaper on Apple M5 Max in on-chip `reported_j`), H5 and H11 on unseen disjoint Form shapes via `FormEBM-v2` (`5.85M` trainable parameters: `AUROC = 0.8922 >= 0.85`, `84.05%` solvable top-1 accuracy, `2.39×` fewer checker calls, and `93.0% >= 80%` strict 5-step cumulative defect monotonicity at Spearman `0.9931` and `47.09 mJ`/candidate core `reported_j`) and span-normalized two-head `FormEBM-v3` (`6.05M` trainable parameters: unseen-shape `H5 AUROC = 0.9437 >= 0.85`, `83.44%` `E_def` and `80.98%` `E_total` solvable top-1 accuracy, `H11` strict 5-step monotonicity `94.0%` on `E_depth` at Spearman `0.9966` and `92.0%` on `E_total` at Spearman `0.9846`, resolving Wave-4 cross-task edit-span inversion on zero-shot `H15` from `0.2585` to `0.5278` pooled AUROC and `0.6018` on large-span valid vs short invalid rewrites at `232.45 mJ`/candidate core `reported_j`), H7 fuzzed arm (0 invariant violations among 1,275 mutants), H8 (0 replay mismatches on 10,500 streams across six targets), H14 (deterministic ladder closing 111 of 151 tasks, 73.5%, at zero model calls, leaving 40 residual tasks), H15 single-shot plus bounded checker-feedback `H15-Iterative` and Shape D (`DNULLMM1`) Gear-0 across those 40 residual tasks (`17/40` to `34/40` single-shot, `22/40` at `k = 1` and `27/40` at `k = 16` for `8.42M` `FormJEPA-v1`, `40/40` closed on `Claude Opus 5.5` and `Claude Sonnet 5.5` with `0` contradictions under `<= 5`-turn checker feedback plus public `stdlib/lib` signature headers, `39/40` on `xAI Grok 4.20 Reasoning`, `40/40` Gear-0 replay at `0` calls and `0.325 µs` per lookup, and `200/200` `Zipf(0.85)` 3-Gear stream admissions at `16.67×` fewer frontier episodes), and the 151-task `rce repair` rung `f` promotion audit (`3/119 = 2.5% > 2.0%`, `3/3` passing `W-MUT-1`, `0` contradictions, confirming rung `f` stays in `observe` mode). Full intent-to-Form synthesis (H3), cross-module signature-and-trace out-of-distribution energy calibration beyond `0.5278`, and external wall-meter energy (`measured_j`) remain unset.
