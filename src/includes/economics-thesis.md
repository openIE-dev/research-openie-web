**Shared thesis, stated once for every paper.** Energy to run is the only true metric of computer intelligence. All other factors collapse to zero. The gate form defines what that means. Among runs that pass the task's completeness predicate $C(z)$ within the stated budget and deadline, systems are ranked by joules to completion, $J^*$. Correctness, deadlines and task size are the gate, not terms in the score. Parameters, tokens, benchmark scores and prices carry zero weight in the ranking ([Agency, Section 1](/papers/agency/#1-thesis)). Estimates are never `measured_j`.

**Price, a separate claim, for the economics companions.** A price trend is a separate claim, labeled as one. Epoch AI reports that the cost of a given level of AI performance has fallen about 47 percent per quarter since 2023, about 13 times per year (Emberson and Roodman, *The plunging price of thought*, 22 September 2026). If that trend continues, energy becomes the dominant marginal cost of inference. That is a cited trend with a condition, not a measured collapse. [Spell Check is Global](/papers/spellcheck/) is an existence proof that some computer intelligence commoditizes to bundled zero price. The theory these companions use is in [the agency paper](/papers/agency/), [Metered Commit Gates](/papers/gates/) and [The Reality Code Engine](/papers/rce/).
