**Shared thesis, stated once for every paper.** Energy to run is the only true metric of computer intelligence. All other factors collapse to zero. The gate form defines what that means. Among runs that pass the task's completeness predicate $C(z)$ within the stated budget and deadline, systems are ranked by joules to completion, $J^*$. Correctness, deadlines and task size are the gate, not terms in the score. Parameters, tokens, benchmark scores and prices carry zero weight in the ranking ([Agency, Section 1](/papers/agency/#1-thesis)). Estimates are never `measured_j`. Prices and markets are economics. They are in the economics companions: [Task Satiation](/papers/satiation/), [Spell Check is Global](/papers/spellcheck/) and [The Promised Trillion Dollar AI-Coding Market](/papers/ai-coding-market/).
