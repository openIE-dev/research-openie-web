RCE Playground

The stack is not fixed. RCE evaluates the entire stack and makes every interface assumption explicit and checked. A correction at any level carries upward and compounds: recursive self-improvement under a gate, where each proven correction is promoted, and model calls and joules fall while correctness holds.

Everything below runs in your browser: the RCE core compiled to wasm32 with wasm-bindgen, no server and no model calls. loading… Read the theory in the RCE paper.
