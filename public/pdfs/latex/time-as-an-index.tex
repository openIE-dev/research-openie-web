\begin{abstract}
\noindent
If a future state of a computational or physical system could be queried directly like an indexed row in a relational database, sequential brute-force search---cryptographic key recovery, hash preimage inversion, and proof-of-work puzzle solving---would stop protecting secrets, because the target witness was never hidden, only unindexed. This study tests that premise across computational complexity theory, quantum foundations, physical relaxation hardware, cryptographic reductions, mechanical proof checking in \texttt{Lean 4.36.0} (\textbf{31 kernel-checked theorems}, zero \texttt{sorry}, standard foundational axioms only), deterministic Python and SymPy models (\texttt{M1--M8} plus pre-registered \texttt{SHA-256} spectral test \texttt{F5}, \texttt{seed = 20261122}), and a unified public ledger of \textbf{195 entries} ($81$ temporal anomalies \texttt{TA001--TA081}, $40$ frequency-atlas edge cases \texttt{AA01--AA40}, and $74$ foundational paradoxes \texttt{P01--P74}). Verdict: \textbf{established inside formal closed-timelike-curve (CTC) models; refuted across known laboratory physics}. With a polynomial Deutsch fixed-point loop (\texttt{D-CTC}), classical and quantum polynomial time both equal $\mathrm{PSPACE}$ (Aaronson \& Watrous 2009), collapsing \texttt{ECDSA}, \texttt{SHA-256d} proof of work, \texttt{AES-256}, and prepare-and-measure quantum key distribution (\texttt{BB84}) in a single fixed-point evaluation while leaving one-time pads unconditionally secure. Without a physical time loop, quantum retrocausal models inherit exact marginal no-signalling, postselected teleportation loops (\texttt{P-CTC}, $\mathrm{PostBQP}=\mathrm{PP}$) pay an inverse-probability repetition bill ($\sim 1/p$, reaching $1/d^2 = 64\dots 4{,}096$ trials on $3\dots 6$-bit witness circuits in \texttt{M2b}), analog relaxation relocates hardness into exponential energy fluctuations or Gabor observation windows $\Delta t \cdot \Delta f \ge 1/(4\pi)$, and every realizable ``future index'' reduces to precomputation bounded by Hellman's $T \cdot M^2 = N^2$ after an $N$-step fill that per-user salts and fresh block hashes (\texttt{0.0\%} reuse in \texttt{M7}) defeat before construction can begin.
\end{abstract}

\section{Formal Time-Loop Models vs.\ Known Physical Indexing}

Deutsch (1991) models a closed timelike curve as a causality-violating register $\rho_{\text{CTC}} \in \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}_{\text{CV}})$ coupled to a chronology-respecting input $\rho_{\text{in}} \in \mathcal{D}(\mathcal{H}_{\text{CR}})$ via unitary $U$, governed by the kinematic fixed-point condition
\begin{equation}
  \rho_{\text{CTC}} = \mathrm{Tr}_{\text{CR}}\!\left[\, U\left(\rho_{\text{in}} \otimes \rho_{\text{CTC}}\right) U^\dagger \,\right], \qquad
  \rho_{\text{out}} = \mathrm{Tr}_{\text{CV}}\!\left[\, U\left(\rho_{\text{in}} \otimes \rho_{\text{CTC}}\right) U^\dagger \,\right].
\end{equation}
Every finite-dimensional channel possesses at least one fixed point, converting deterministic contradictions into mixed states while making $\rho_{\text{in}} \mapsto \rho_{\text{out}}$ \emph{nonlinear}. \end{enumerate}
