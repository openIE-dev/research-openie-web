\section{0. Overview}\label{overview}

When two agent evaluations report different task-completion rates or
different energy figures, the gap frequently comes from how the model
was wrapped and how the host counters were attributed rather than from
the model itself. Changing only the surrounding harness shifted
\texttt{Gemini\ 2.\allowbreak{}5\ Pro} task resolution by \texttt{17} percentage
points on \texttt{Terminal-\allowbreak{}Bench\ 2}, while subtracting a drifted idle
baseline or splitting a shared silicon rail by processor time can
distort energy attribution by more than \texttt{30\%}.

This study specifies and empirically verifies the \textbf{Satiation
Benchmark (\texttt{SATB})} evaluation contract: one fixed sequential
Rust reference harness (\textbf{\texttt{Lane\ B}}), one proxy-isolated
adapter bridge (\textbf{\texttt{Shapes\ D,\allowbreak{}\ M,\allowbreak{}\ H,\allowbreak{}\ and\ I}}) for
zero-model controls, raw model endpoints, external command-line
harnesses, and public benchmark solvers, a five-block system-under-test
(\texttt{SUT}) cryptographic fingerprint, twelve mandatory conformance
checks, and a five-level rootless telemetry engine. Rather than leaving
the design unmeasured, we execute live conformance and falsifier
benchmarks across \textbf{five hardware archetypes} spanning
\texttt{macOS}, \texttt{Linux}, and \texttt{Windows\ 11}: an
\texttt{Apple\ M5\ Max} workstation (\texttt{128} gigabytes of unified
memory, \texttt{macOS\ 27.\allowbreak{}2.\allowbreak{}1}, on-chip \texttt{IOReport} and kernel
\texttt{proc\_\allowbreak{}pid\_\allowbreak{}rusage} \texttt{reported\_\allowbreak{}j}), a Tegra
\texttt{aarch64} developer kit (\texttt{Jetson\ Thor},
\texttt{Linux\ 6.\allowbreak{}8.\allowbreak{}12-\allowbreak{}1021-\allowbreak{}tegra}, physical Texas Instruments
whole-board and three-channel rail-separated current-shunt
\texttt{measured\_\allowbreak{}j}), a \texttt{Framework} Desktop \texttt{Strix\ Halo}
workstation (\texttt{Ryzen\ Max+\ 395},
\texttt{Linux\ 7.\allowbreak{}0.\allowbreak{}0-\allowbreak{}43-\allowbreak{}generic\ x86-\allowbreak{}64}, \texttt{0440\ root:energy}
group-isolated package-energy \texttt{reported\_\allowbreak{}j} plus \texttt{amdgpu}
package-power tracking), a \texttt{Raptor\ Lake} workstation
(\texttt{Intel\ Core\ i9-\allowbreak{}13900H},
\texttt{Linux\ 7.\allowbreak{}0.\allowbreak{}0-\allowbreak{}43-\allowbreak{}generic\ x86-\allowbreak{}64}, platform and package energy
\texttt{reported\_\allowbreak{}j}), and a bare-metal \texttt{Windows\ 11} workstation
(\texttt{Intel\ Core\ i7-\allowbreak{}1165G7} plus \texttt{GeForce\ 2060},
\texttt{Windows\ 11} build \texttt{26300} \texttt{x86-64}, rootless
cumulative discrete-graphics \texttt{reported\_\allowbreak{}j}, Win32 Job Object
confinement, and unprivileged processor-package energy strictly marked
\texttt{not\_\allowbreak{}available}), alongside closed cloud server energy
(\texttt{not\_\allowbreak{}available}).

\textbf{In plain words}

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  An agent's prompt loop, context-trimming rule, and tool sandbox can
  swing benchmark scores as much as swapping the underlying model, so
  \texttt{Lane\ B} holds that wrapper completely fixed in deterministic
  Rust while \texttt{Lane\ O} forces external wrappers through one
  audited proxy plug.
\item
  Energy channels are recorded raw at their true physical boundary ---
  whole wall plug, board input, chip package, operating-system process
  estimate, or logical token ledger --- and are never blended into a
  single unlabelled number, summed across evidence classes
  (\texttt{measured\_\allowbreak{}j} vs.~\texttt{reported\_\allowbreak{}j} vs.~\texttt{est\_j}),
  or back-filled with fleet sustainability averages.
\item
  Live tests across five silicon and operating-system platforms
  (\texttt{macOS}, \texttt{Linux}, and \texttt{Windows\ 11}) verify
  exact \texttt{Shape\ D} million-record memory-mapped
  (\texttt{15027705736}) and \texttt{C9} replay (\texttt{149,\allowbreak{}747\ B},
  \texttt{0} newline or byte diffs) determinism, \texttt{0.\allowbreak{}87–9.\allowbreak{}54\ ms}
  kernel process-tree cleanup (\texttt{2.46\ ms} via Win32 Job Objects
  on \texttt{Windows\ 11}), cloud \texttt{temperature\ =\ 0}
  non-determinism across three vendors, hardware sensor cadence ceilings
  (\texttt{24.\allowbreak{}3–45.\allowbreak{}4\ µs} Linux counter reads at \texttt{94–97\ Hz}
  vs.~\texttt{1.17\ ms} Windows in-process graphics-counter reads at
  \texttt{64.6\ Hz} vs.~\texttt{2.95\ ms} serial-bus shunt reads at
  \texttt{66.6\ Hz} on \texttt{Jetson\ Thor}
  vs.~\texttt{\textasciitilde{}8\ Hz} \texttt{macmon}
  \texttt{IOReport}), \texttt{32.\allowbreak{}59\%–89.\allowbreak{}88\%} error when splitting
  sequential compute-plus-storage phases by processor time
  (\texttt{Falsifier\ 3}), and physical graphics-versus-processor shunt
  rail separation alongside \texttt{Falsifier\ 6} shared-host drift
  gating (\texttt{dedicated\_\allowbreak{}host\ =\ false}).
\end{enumerate}

closing Open Questions \texttt{OQ-1} and \texttt{OQ-2}:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Holding the wrapper constant (\texttt{Lane\ B}) vs.~evaluating
  external wrappers (\texttt{Lane\ O})}: \texttt{Lane\ B}
  (\texttt{satb-\allowbreak{}agent}) fixes a single auditable Rust loop across all
  models, whereas \texttt{Lane\ O} wraps complete third-party harnesses
  (\texttt{Claude\ Code}, \texttt{Codex\ CLI}, \texttt{OpenHands},
  \texttt{Terminus-\allowbreak{}2}, \texttt{Inspect}) as black-box \texttt{SUT}s
  whose internal prompts and compaction calls are hashed into the
  \texttt{harness} fingerprint block and routed 100\% through
  \texttt{satb-\allowbreak{}proxy}.
\item
  \textbf{Context policy resolution (\texttt{OQ-1}:
  \texttt{ContextRule\_\allowbreak{}v0})}: Hard-killing an attempt with zero elision
  conflates multi-step reasoning with verbose tool output, whereas
  \texttt{LLM}-generated summarization (\texttt{Claude\ Code}
  auto-compact) injects hidden stochastic model calls that break
  deterministic harness replay. \texttt{Lane\ B} adopts
  \textbf{\texttt{ContextRule\_\allowbreak{}v0}}: whenever accumulated conversation
  size exceeds \textbf{\texttt{80\%}} of the declared context window,
  the harness deterministically replaces the oldest tool outputs with
  \texttt{"{[}ELIDED\_BY\_CONTEXT\_RULE\_V0{]}"} while keeping
  \texttt{system(\allowbreak{}TEMPLATE\_\allowbreak{}v0)}, the initial
  \texttt{user(\allowbreak{}render(\allowbreak{}bundle))}, and the \textbf{most recent \texttt{8}
  tool outputs} verbatim, making \textbf{zero model calls}. Hard
  \texttt{killed(\allowbreak{}context\_\allowbreak{}ceiling)} fires only if those retained
  messages alone exceed \texttt{100\%} of the window.
\item
  \textbf{Tool surface resolution (\texttt{OQ-2}: \texttt{TOOLS\_v0})}:
  Pure \texttt{exec}-only (\texttt{mini}-style) forces models to
  shell-escape multi-line source patches and algebraic proofs
  (\texttt{cat\ \textless{}\textless{}\ \textquotesingle{}EOF\textquotesingle{}}),
  introducing shell-quoting failures orthogonal to task reasoning and
  complicating portable rootless sandboxing across \texttt{macOS},
  \texttt{Linux}, and \texttt{Windows}. \texttt{Lane\ B} fixes
  \textbf{\texttt{TOOLS\_v0}} --- four workspace tools (\texttt{exec},
  \texttt{read\_\allowbreak{}file}, \texttt{write\_\allowbreak{}file}, \texttt{list\_dir}) plus
  two terminal tools (\texttt{submit}, \texttt{refuse}), canonicalized
  via \texttt{JCS} into \texttt{tools\_\allowbreak{}hash} (\texttt{d5b102...}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{3.2 Canonical four-way outcome mapping
(\texttt{OQ-3})}{3.2 Canonical four-way outcome mapping (OQ-3)}}\label{canonical-four-way-outcome-mapping-oq-3}

To prevent benchmarks from silently dropping crashed runs or conflating
voluntary bad answers with infrastructure timeouts, \texttt{SATB} maps
legacy five-state completion codes losslessly onto
\textbf{\texttt{\{pass,\ fail,\ refuse,\ killed\}}} (\texttt{PFRK}),
preserving the exact \texttt{sub\_\allowbreak{}reason} in every receipt:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{completed} \(\to\) \texttt{pass}
  (\texttt{sub\_\allowbreak{}reason:\ "verified"}, score \texttt{1})}: \texttt{SUT}
  called \texttt{submit(\allowbreak{}bytes)} prior to ceiling expiration and the
  external \texttt{Verifier} accepted \texttt{bytes} after
  \texttt{attempt.\allowbreak{}close}.
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{incorrect} \(\to\) \texttt{fail}
  (\texttt{sub\_\allowbreak{}reason:\ "incorrect"}, score \texttt{0})}: \texttt{SUT}
  called \texttt{submit(\allowbreak{}bytes)} cleanly; external \texttt{Verifier}
  rejected the candidate.
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{malformed} \(\to\) \texttt{fail}
  (\texttt{sub\_\allowbreak{}reason:\ "malformed\_\allowbreak{}submission"}, score \texttt{0})}:
  \texttt{SUT} voluntarily called \texttt{submit(\allowbreak{}bytes)} with
  unparseable or schema-invalid content. A bad submission authored by
  the \texttt{SUT} is an accuracy failure, never an infrastructure kill.
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{refused} \(\to\) \texttt{refuse}
  (\texttt{sub\_reason:\ "policy"\ \textbar{}\ "capability"\ \textbar{}\ "other"},
  score \texttt{0})}: \texttt{SUT} explicitly invoked
  \texttt{refuse(\allowbreak{}reason)}.
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{no\_\allowbreak{}submission} \(\to\) \texttt{killed}
  (\texttt{sub\_reason:\ "wall\_ceiling"\ \textbar{}\ "token\_ceiling"\ \textbar{}\ "turn\_ceiling"\ \textbar{}\ "context\_ceiling"\ \textbar{}\ "usage\_missing"\ \textbar{}\ "exec\_timeout\_killed"\ \textbar{}\ "process\_crash"},
  score \texttt{0})}: \texttt{satb-\allowbreak{}supervisor} terminated the attempt
  before any terminal tool call arrived. All four outcomes remain in the
  denominator \(n\).
\end{enumerate}

Every energy value in a receipt carries one of seven attribution labels:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{A1} (Exclusive physical rail)}: A hardware rail sensor
  touched exclusively by one partition during the measurement window
  (e.g., \texttt{Jetson\ Thor\ INA3221\ ch1\ VDD\_\allowbreak{}GPU} when only the
  local inference engine has \texttt{GPU} device access).
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{A2} (Dedicated whole-system boundary)}: A
  whole-machine (\texttt{L0}/\allowbreak{}\texttt{L1}) or whole-package (\texttt{L2})
  counter on a quiesced dedicated machine (\texttt{SUT} plus idle
  \texttt{OS} floor). \textbf{Per \texttt{OQ-4}, whole-\texttt{SUT} raw
  window energy at \texttt{A2} is the primary physical energy metric for
  placement}, preventing thin models from offloading uncounted
  brute-force search into \texttt{/work} subprocesses and preventing
  \texttt{active\ -\allowbreak{}\ idle} subtraction from flattering slow runs.
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{B} (Temporal phase split)}: Counter differences across
  non-overlapping time windows delimited by sequential phase markers
  (\texttt{model\_\allowbreak{}call} vs.~\texttt{tool\_\allowbreak{}call} in \texttt{Lane\ B}).
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{C} (Proportional share --- the \texttt{Kepler}
  model)}: A shared hardware counter partitioned by
  \(\Delta \text{CPU}_{\text{partition}} / \Delta \text{CPU}_{\text{total}}\)
  or \texttt{GPU} utilization when parallel processes overlap
  (\texttt{Lane\ O} \texttt{Shape\ H}).
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{D} (\texttt{OS}-modelled estimate)}: The kernel's
  internal per-process energy model (\texttt{macOS\ ri\_\allowbreak{}energy\_\allowbreak{}nj}).
  Isolates a process tree from \texttt{CPU} co-tenants, but remains a
  firmware/kernel model rather than a physical shunt reading.
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{P} (Provider-attested)}: A per-request server energy
  number returned by a cloud provider alongside its verbatim measurement
  methodology.
\item
  \textbf{\texttt{N} (\texttt{not\_\allowbreak{}available})}: Unobserved and
  \textbf{never estimated}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{5.2 Seven-step counter discipline, rootless
collection (\texttt{OQ-5}), and platform
maps}{5.2 Seven-step counter discipline, rootless collection (OQ-5), and platform maps}}\label{seven-step-counter-discipline-rootless-collection-oq-5-and-platform-maps}

\texttt{satb-\allowbreak{}meter} processes every attempt through seven ordered steps:

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Read cumulative counters at every event boundary}
  (\texttt{attempt.\allowbreak{}start}, each \texttt{model\_\allowbreak{}call} and
  \texttt{tool\_\allowbreak{}call}, \texttt{attempt.\allowbreak{}close}, plus a \texttt{100\ ms}
  reference tick) so window energy is an exact counter difference
  \(\Delta E = E(t_{\text{end}}) - E(t_{\text{start}})\).
\item
  \textbf{Write raw channels first}: record boundary, sensor, start/end
  readings, counter wraps, and sample count; mark windows shorter than
  \texttt{10} sensor periods \texttt{"below\_\allowbreak{}resolution"} and absent
  hardware \texttt{"not\_\allowbreak{}available"}.
\item
  \textbf{Split sequential phases (\texttt{B})}: in \texttt{Lane\ B},
  model inference and \texttt{/work} tool calls never overlap; parallel
  \texttt{Lane\ O} harnesses drop phase splits to \texttt{C} or
  \texttt{N}.
\item
  \textbf{Isolate \texttt{engine\_\allowbreak{}resident\_\allowbreak{}during\_\allowbreak{}tools}}: while a
  \texttt{/work} tool runs, a local model server (\texttt{llama-\allowbreak{}server},
  \texttt{vLLM}) holds weights resident on the
  \texttt{GPU}/\allowbreak{}\texttt{DRAM}. Its idle draw during the tool window is
  placed in \texttt{engine\_\allowbreak{}resident\_\allowbreak{}during\_\allowbreak{}tools} and charged neither
  to model active inference nor to tool execution.
\item
  \textbf{Split by proportion (\texttt{C}) only where partitions
  overlap}: apply \(\Delta \text{CPU}\) ratios strictly as a
  characterisation fallback, recognizing that \texttt{C}-states, turbo
  \texttt{DVFS}, and \texttt{DRAM}/\allowbreak{}storage stalls violate
  proportionality.
\item
  \textbf{Store baselines as derived channels}: bracket sessions with
  pre/post resident baselines; if pre/post idle power drifts beyond
  \texttt{5.0\%} (\texttt{Falsifier\ 6}), drop derived net-above-idle
  values while keeping raw channels intact.
\item
  \textbf{Run the sum check (\texttt{Falsifier\ 2})}: verify that
  derived phase components sum back to the raw attempt counter delta
  within one sensor quantum (\texttt{1\ mJ}); if they do not, discard
  the derived split and retain only the raw channel.
\end{enumerate}

Consequently, \textbf{seven quantities cannot be honestly attributed}
and are barred from \texttt{SATB} claims: (1) unattested
closed-\texttt{API} server energy (\texttt{N} or \texttt{P} only); (2)
exact per-process physical joules on a shared \texttt{SoC} or
unified-memory rail (\texttt{C} and \texttt{D} are models); (3) model
weights isolated from serving kernels, quantization, batching, and
silicon; (4) ownership of the \texttt{OS} idle floor and
\texttt{DRAM}-resident weight power (reported as standalone channels);
(5) individual events shorter than the sensor period (such as a
\texttt{30\ ms} tool call under a \texttt{1\ s} plug); (6) shared cloud
\texttt{VM}s or unprivileged Windows \texttt{CPU} domains lacking
user-mode hardware counters; and (7) silent mid-session provider weight
swaps (flagged by start/end canaries, never ruled out a priori). In
particular (\texttt{OQ-6}), \textbf{provider sustainability disclosures
are fleet medians rather than per-request measurements and never enter a
\texttt{SATB} receipt} --- including Google's \texttt{0.24\ Wh} median
\texttt{Gemini\ Apps} prompt, OpenAI's \texttt{0.34\ Wh} figure
(published without methodology), and Mistral's \texttt{1.\allowbreak{}14\ gCO2e} per
\texttt{400}-token response.
