Spell Check is Global

AI is going to follow the path of all computer automation. Spell check is the completed example. Energy to run is the only true metric. What remains is joules.

Teaching figures for /papers/spellcheck/. The curve below is a labeled illustration of cited endpoints. No coefficient is estimated. Estimates are not package measured_j. board_synth_claimed=false. The many (7B+) and the few (under 500M) are an access framing, not a census.

sc-tok-01 One token, taught in order

Norvig's documented call

correction('speling') returns spelling in Norvig's essay. The steps are the companion laws on a finished path. Lookup, then a short edit. The suggestion is a proposal until commit. Further candidates stop when the token is kept. A frontier model that also says "spelling" has not produced a better word. It has produced the same word at a money price commoditization removes. His reported final test, 68 percent of 400 pairs at 35 words per second, is the toy's score, not an industrial claim.

StepWhat happensWhich study
1The string is already on the machine.Automation path
2Is it in the local list? If not, try edit distance one.Mixture of Limits
3Show the suggestion. Write it only on acceptance.Notational Intelligence
4Once kept, more candidates do not change the predicate.Satiation
5The invoice can be zero. The probe still spends joules.Metabolic Intelligence
