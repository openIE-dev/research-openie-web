sc-tok-01 One token, taught in order

Norvig's documented call

correction('speling') returns spelling in Norvig's essay. The steps are the companion laws on a finished path. Lookup, then a short edit. The suggestion is a proposal until commit. Further candidates stop when the token is kept. A frontier model that also says "spelling" has not produced a better word. It has produced the same word at a money price commoditization removes. His reported final test, 68 percent of 400 pairs at 35 words per second, is the toy's score, not an industrial claim.

1The string is already on the machine.Automation path
