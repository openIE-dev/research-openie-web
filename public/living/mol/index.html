mol-tab-01 How Mixture of Limits differs from MoE

Short contrast from paper §9. Field systems optimize which neural generator runs. Mixture of Limits differs from Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) in the decision it makes: it asks whether generation should run at all. The full model-to-model SOTA table and verified cites live in the study. No board joules are asserted here.
