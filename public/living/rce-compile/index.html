rc-diag-02 · Live E5 De Bruijn Name-Free Hash vs. Naive Identifier-Strip Comparator

Exact In-Browser SHA-256

Why does Compiler Form Part R require positional / De Bruijn binder indices rather than simply stripping identifier names from an AST? In E5-Scale (185 W1 RCE Forms + 60 C/Rust/Python kernels + 500 verified semantic mutants), naive AST identifier-stripping suffered 150 / 150 (100.0%) false-positive cache collisions on variable-binding swaps (x - y → y - x), whereas De Bruijn nameless hashing achieved 0 / 500 (0.00%) false hits and cut cold compilation reported_j by 167.8× (clang -O3) and 448.5× (rustc -O3).
