"AI" Reads Human: Format Impedance & reported_j Observatory

David Charlot, Open Interface Engineering · Companion to /papers/ai-reads-human/

Measurement grammar on this page. Every table and figure separates three evidence classes: Exact (Bytes & Tokens) for seeded corpus byte sizes, SHA-256 file digests, and exact BPE token counts across five open tokenizers; Reported (reported_j: L3 & L2) for Apple M5 Max per-process kernel proc_pid_rusage(RUSAGE_INFO_V6).ri_energy_nj (L3, label D, 34 workloads × 5 shuffled replicates = 170 windows) paired with whole-SoC macmon IOReport (L2, label A2, where Falsifier 6 invalidated 109 / 170 windows due to > 5.0% shared-host idle drift); and Scaled Projection for corpus-volume extrapolation. measured_j = None; board_metered = false; GPU prefill/decode joules remain planned (M4).
